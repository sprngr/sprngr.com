The following is a collection of fan art game tokens I have produced during the second half of 2025. Most of these were available as short print run rewards on my [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/sprngr).

## Lander
When Edge of Eternities dropped, I wanted to take a stab at a Lander design. I had fun leaning into that sci-fi hardware aesthetic.

![](/img/visual-works/tokens-2025-h2/lander.png)

## Chili Dog
When the Sonic the Hedgehog Secret Lair dropped, I was beside myself as a long time fan. Naturally I had to do a chili dog.

![](/img/visual-works/tokens-2025-h2/chili-dog.png)

## The Skeleman Cometh
A highly requested 4/1 Skeleton with menace for October. I also participated in the Tokentober art challenge while I was at it.

![](/img/visual-works/tokens-2025-h2/skeleman.png)

## Halloween Drop
A little extra for everyone in the October mailer.

![](/img/visual-works/tokens-2025-h2/halloween-drop.png)

## Bender
With the Avatar set introducing four bending mechanics, one per element, it would only be right to make a Bender token.

![](/img/visual-works/tokens-2025-h2/bender.png)

## Dog
The December patron drop, hopefully it warms you on those cold winter days.

![](/img/visual-works/tokens-2025-h2/dog.png)
