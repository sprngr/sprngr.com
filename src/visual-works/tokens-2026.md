The following is a collection of fan art game tokens I have produced during 2026. Most of these were available as short print run rewards on my [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/sprngr).

## Dungeons & Doglins
As predicted, Lorwyn Eclipsed was packed full of Lorwyn/Shadowmoor goodness - so naturally I had to make a goblin. I also experimented with a mimic that used a foil finish to create a mirror effect in print.

![](/img/visual-works/tokens-2026/dungeon_doglins.png)

## Fleem Mart Collection
A collection of store coupon inspired tokens, debuted at Shoeobox 2026. A fitting conclusion to the Fleem-Mart series with the Employee of the Month Goblin spawned from doodles at Shoebox.

![](/img/visual-works/tokens-2026/fleem-mart_1.png)
![](/img/visual-works/tokens-2026/fleem-mart_2.png)

A set of mana tracker tokens to keep you cool during the hot summer months.

## Non Fungi-ble Saprolings
July's saproling tokens, NFT style. Blockchain is some sort of cube format, right? The entire set was done as 55 unique tokens with a combination of illustration, color, and hash that are hand serialized.

![](/img/visual-works/tokens-2026/saprolings_1.png)
![](/img/visual-works/tokens-2026/saprolings_2.png)

## Goblin Army
Inspired by the cover for the Dutch translation of The Hobbit - amassing goblins for August.

![](/img/visual-works/tokens-2026/goblin_army.png)

## Gummi Wurmcoil Engine
Keeping with my candy inspired theme for October, I acted upon an idea rolling around in my head for years with one of my favorite cards - Wurmcoil Engine. I keep the promo I got from Scars of Mirrodin in my EDH deck despite it not completely working with the rest of the theme.

![](/img/visual-works/tokens-2026/gummi_wurms.png)
